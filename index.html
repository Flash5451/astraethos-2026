    06 // Protocol

    Rules & Participant Guide

    Short, strict, and fair. Read once before launch day — the mission runs on these.

    Rules of Engagement

    • R·01Teams of exactly 2 members.
    • R·02Maximum 25 teams / 50 participants on the roster.
    • R·03Every team participates in every phase — no phase-skipping.
    • R·04All answers are submitted from the team's seat; no buzzers.
    • R·05Scores are cumulative across all three phases.
    • R·06Phase A: 20 written questions × 3 points each.
    • R·07Phase B: written answers to visual-puzzle rebus sets.
    • R·08Phase C: rover concept sheet + rapid pitch.
    • R·09No physical rover prototype or coding is required.

    Tie-Breaker Protocol

    Applied in strict order until the tie is resolved:

    • T·01Higher Phase C score.
    • T·02Higher Phase B total.
    • T·03Higher Tier-3 puzzle subtotal in Phase B.
    • T·04Higher Phase C pitch score.
    • T·05Joint rank declared by the judges' panel.

    How do we register?

    Through the official Google Form — the Register button at the top of the page, the one in the Registration section and the one in the footer all open the same form. Fill in both crew members' details; your slot is confirmed by the mission team over email or phone.

    Do we need to build an actual robot?

    No. Phase C is a pure design challenge — a rover concept sheet plus a rapid pitch. No prototype, no hardware, no coding required.

    Who can participate?

    Any student team of exactly two members, from any department and any year. Cross-department crews are encouraged — the best rovers need more than one discipline.

    What should we bring on event day?

    A pen and your sharpest pattern recognition. Answer sheets, puzzle packs and briefing materials are provided at registration. Reach Gallery Hall (Room No: 1601) by 08:30 for check-in.

    Is there a registration fee?

    No fee. Slots are capped at 25 teams and fill on a first-come, first-served basis — transmit early.

    Can we choose our phase timings?

    No. The mission runs on a fixed flight plan. Every team competes in every phase at the scheduled time.

    How are ties resolved?

    By the five-step Tie-Breaker Protocol listed in the Rules section — applied in strict order by the judges' panel.
